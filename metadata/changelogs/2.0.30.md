### Added

- Biodiesel, refined from Oil in the Mixer. Burns hotter and longer in the Combustion Dynamo. Idea from heresiax (breakinblocks/Nautec#46).
- Crude Glycerin, a Biodiesel byproduct that mixes into Etching Acid.
- Combustion Dynamo fuels are datapack-driven (`nautec:combustion_fuels`), replacing the oil config options.

### Changed

- Structures are saved in the 26.1.2 format, so world generation no longer converts them on load.

### Fixed

- Breaking the Bacterial Analyzer's microscope voided the analyzer (breakinblocks/Nautec#48).
- Breaking a formed Deep Sea Drain wall voided it (breakinblocks/Nautec#47).
